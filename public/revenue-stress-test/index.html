A deliberately simple downside view

This worksheet reduces both nightly rate and booked nights, then increases your total cost estimate. The model treats costs as one annual amount; it does not automatically reduce variable expenses when bookings fall. Adjust the entered costs if that assumption does not fit your property.

Read this as a scenario

Multiplying two reductions compounds the effect. A 15% rate reduction and 20% booked-night reduction produce a 32% revenue reduction, not 35%. This is arithmetic under your inputs, not a prediction of market performance.
