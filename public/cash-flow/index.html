What to enter

Include recurring costs such as management, utilities, insurance, property taxes, platform fees, maintenance and net cleaning costs in annual operating costs. Keep their treatment consistent with the room revenue entered. Total cash invested should include your down payment, closing costs, initial work, setup, fees and reserves.

How to read the result

Revenue is nightly rate × booked nights. The loan assumes a fully amortizing fixed-rate loan with monthly payments. Cash flow subtracts operating costs and principal-and-interest payments. It excludes income taxes, appreciation, loan fees, irregular capital work and changes to reserves. Use the lender’s quote for financing decisions.
