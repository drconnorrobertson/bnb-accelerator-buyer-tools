Keep fixed and variable costs separate

Fixed costs can include loan payments, property taxes, insurance and costs you expect even with no bookings. Variable costs change with revenue or occupancy. Enter a percentage only when it reasonably represents the costs in your scenario. Do not include the same cost in both fields.

Formula

Nightly contribution = nightly rate × (1 − variable cost share). Required booked nights = fixed annual costs ÷ nightly contribution, rounded up. Required occupancy divides those nights by available nights. A result above 100% means the scenario cannot cover the entered costs within its available nights.
