Cash-flow worksheet

Annual revenue = nightly rate × booked nights. Monthly interest r = annual percentage ÷ 1200; n = amortization years × 12. For a nonzero rate, monthly payment = principal × r ÷ (1 − (1+r)−n). At zero interest, payment = principal ÷ n. Annual debt service = monthly payment × 12.

Cash flow = revenue − operating costs − debt service. Cash-on-cash percentage = cash flow ÷ total cash invested × 100. The outputs are pre-tax and do not establish an investment recommendation.

Break-even worksheet

Nightly contribution = rate × (1 − variable percentage ÷ 100). Required nights = ceiling(fixed costs ÷ contribution). Required occupancy = required nights ÷ available nights × 100. The model highlights when required nights exceed available nights.

Stress test

Base revenue = rate × booked nights. Stressed revenue = base revenue × (1 − rate reduction ÷ 100) × (1 − booked-night reduction ÷ 100). Stressed cash flow subtracts costs increased by the selected percentage. Costs are held as an annual aggregate, not an occupancy-sensitive schedule.

Limits and data handling

Calculations run locally with no application submission or input transmission. Hosting providers may process ordinary request logs. Worksheets do not incorporate income taxes, property eligibility, local restrictions, appreciation, capital expenditure schedules or variable interest rates. Results are rounded for display; calculations retain full precision internally.

Example inputs are hypothetical and are not BNB customer results. Contact the company through its main website for service information.
